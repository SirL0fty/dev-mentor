Fixes for the mistakes a real look-back found most often. Acronyms and domain terms are spelled out at every level (with an optional project glossary), questions get a one-line answer first, and claims about data or deploys come with how to see it yourself. Lessons show which repo, branch and folder you're in, and admit when they contradict themselves. New files come with a link you can open, and shareable text never has local paths. Pasted errors get a plain-words card with the likeliest cause and one check. `coach` lists the files it will touch before editing, "just tell me" holds until you say go, `explain` teaches the editor moves to follow code yourself, and `share` drafts short Slack replies, standup updates and questions for a senior in your voice.
