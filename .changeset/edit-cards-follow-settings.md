`coach` edit cards keep their shape for the whole lesson. Every edit card, including a fix, shows where, **Before** (the file's real lines, re-read each time), **After** in your build mode and why: with `build: me` the After shows the shape with your part as a hint. Every card is checked against your current settings, so long lessons don't drift and settings changes apply from the next card.
