Lessons teach more like a good senior. Flow maps grow one part per card at junior and below, you're sometimes invited to predict the next value before it's shown, and every passing check says what it doesn't prove. "Why was it built like this?" is answered from the commits and PRs behind the code. `coach` traces bugs to where they start and writes the failing test first, ends features with a self-review checklist, and checks the goal with you when the ticket doesn't say why. `review` picks at most two review lenses from what the diff touches and can cite your own coding standards (add `standards: <path>` to your profile). New `plain words` and `catch me up` commands, and plainer cards with no "key insight" labels.
