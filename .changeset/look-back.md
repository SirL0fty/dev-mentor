New `look-back` skill: it reads your past chats from any assistant (T3 Code, OpenCode, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or chats you paste) after you choose the scope, finds what you keep asking and correcting, and turns each pattern into a lesson or a lasting fix that you approve first. Say `look back monthly` to have the agent run it on a schedule where your assistant has one. Between runs, Dev Mentor fixes a correction you give twice and offers the lasting fix there and then, and mentions an unread or month-old look-back report.
