# share

[Skill file](../../skills/share/SKILL.md)

## What it does

`share` writes up the current plan or lesson as a document a colleague or lead can review without the chat, with specific questions for them. It saves the document only.

## When to reach for it

Say "share this plan with my senior". To turn an agreed plan into a spec, use `spec`.

## It's working if

- The document asks 2–4 specific questions, not "any thoughts?".
- Steps are marked proposed, done or checked, and only things actually run are called checked.
- It saves to the place you ask (file, Obsidian, Notion) or `docs/plans/`, reads it back, and gives the location.
- Asked to "send this to my lead", it saves the document and says it didn't send it.
